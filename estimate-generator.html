Frequently asked questions

Is an estimate legally binding?

Generally an estimate is an offer rather than a contract, and it becomes binding when the client accepts it on the stated terms. Wording matters, so if the number could move, say so on the document and say why.

What is the difference between an estimate and a quote?

In everyday use a quote is a firm price and an estimate is an approximation that may change. Both use the same document; what differs is what you promise in the notes.

How long should an estimate stay valid?

Long enough for the client to decide, short enough that your material costs have not moved — 14 to 30 days is the usual range. Set it in the “Valid for” field so it prints on the document.
