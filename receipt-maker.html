Free Receipt Maker

A receipt proves a payment already happened, which is why it is stamped PAID rather than carrying a due date. Fill in what was bought and how it was paid for, then print it or save it as a PDF.

How it works

  1. Fill in your details and the client's. They are remembered in this browser for next time.
  2. Add a line for each item or task, with a quantity and a rate.
  3. Set tax, any discount, and the terms. The totals recalculate as you type.
  4. Press Print / Save as PDF and choose Save as PDF.
Your data stays yours. This tool makes no network requests. What you type is held in your browser's local storage on your own machine, so closing the tab does not lose it — and clearing your browser data removes it entirely.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a receipt and an invoice?

An invoice asks for money; a receipt confirms money arrived. If the client has not paid yet, use the invoice generator instead. See invoice vs receipt vs estimate for the full comparison.

Do I have to give a receipt?

Rules vary by country and by transaction type, but issuing one is good practice regardless: it closes the loop for the client and gives you both a matching record at tax time.

Can I use this for cash payments?

Yes. Cash is the case where a receipt matters most, because there is no bank record backing it up. Put “Cash” in the payment method field.
