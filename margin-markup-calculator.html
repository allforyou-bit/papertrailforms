Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Both measure the same profit, against different bases. Margin is profit divided by the selling price; markup is profit divided by the cost. Markup is always the larger number.

Which one should I price with?

Price with markup, because you start from a cost you know. Report with margin, because that is what the profit is as a share of the revenue that appears on your books.

Why does a 100% margin break the maths?

A 100% margin means profit equals the selling price, which means the cost is zero. There is no finite price that satisfies it for a non-zero cost, so the calculator caps the input just below 100%.
